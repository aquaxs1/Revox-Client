FAQ

Good to know

Is Revox safe to use?

Yes. Revox starts games through Roblox's official launch link and reads only public Roblox data. It works alongside the Roblox client rather than modifying it.

Do I need to log in?

No. There is no sign-in at all. For the friends list you just confirm which Roblox profile is yours, or pick it from a search with avatars.

Where is my data stored?

In a local database on your PC. There is no Revox server and nothing gets uploaded. You can export your sessions to CSV or JSON any time.

Can I join my friends' games?

Yes. When a friend's server is public, their entry gets a Join button that drops you straight in.

How does playtime tracking work?

Turn it on once and Revox records a session every time you finish playing, then builds your charts and rankings from it.

Which systems are supported?

Revox is built for Windows and installs for your user account in seconds, no admin rights needed.

Does this website use cookies?

No cookies and no tracking. See the privacy page for the details.
