The Revox client

  • Your data stays on your PC. Library, profiles, sessions, statistics, watchlist and settings live in a local database (revox-client.sqlite in %APPDATA%\app.revox.client). There is no Revox server, so Sebi receives none of it. Deleting that folder deletes this data.
  • Public Roblox data, no login. To show games, profiles, friends, servers and items, the client asks Roblox's public web APIs (*.roblox.com) directly from your PC, without signing in: no password, no .ROBLOSECURITY cookie, no token. Roblox receives your IP address and what is looked up, such as a Place ID, a user ID or a search term. Pictures are loaded from Roblox's image servers (rbxcdn.com). Roblox's privacy policy applies.
  • In the background, only for what you set up: with friend notifications on, the client asks Roblox every minute what the friends of your linked profile are doing, and everything on your watchlist is looked up every six hours.
  • Linking your Roblox profile. The suggested profile comes from the user ID that Roblox writes into its own log files on your PC (%LOCALAPPDATA%\Roblox\logs). The client reads it locally and asks you to confirm. The ID is then only used for the Roblox lookups above.
  • Playtime tracking is off until you turn it on. It then watches for the Roblox process on your PC and writes your sessions to the local database. Exports to CSV or JSON happen only when you ask for them, to a file you pick.
  • Discord Rich Presence is off by default. Switched on, the client passes the game's name, your local profile's name and the start time to the Discord app on your PC, which shows them on your Discord profile. No Discord token is stored. Discord's privacy policy applies.
  • Update check. Only when you click Check for updates in Settings does the client fetch the release information from the project's GitHub releases. GitHub receives your IP address; nothing from your library, profiles or settings is sent.
  • No telemetry. The client sends no usage statistics, crash reports or analytics anywhere.
