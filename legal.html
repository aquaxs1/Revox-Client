Privacy policy

This website

  • No cookies, no tracking. This site sets no cookies, uses no analytics and embeds no ad networks.
  • Server logs. The hosting provider processes technically necessary connection data such as IP address, time and requested page in order to deliver and secure the site (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Downloads. The download page fetches version information from GitHub and the files themselves are served by GitHub, which receives your IP address in the process. GitHub's privacy statement applies.

The Revox client

  • Your data stays local. Library, profiles, sessions, statistics and watchlist live in a local SQLite database on your device. There is no Revox server and nothing is transmitted to us.
  • Public Roblox data. The app reads public Roblox APIs without signing in, so there are no passwords or login cookies involved.
  • Playtime tracking is a setting you turn on yourself and can switch off again at any time. Exports to CSV or JSON happen only when you ask for them.
  • Discord Rich Presence is optional and stores no Discord token.
